Consumption in port (t/day)

How it is calculated

Leg days = distance ÷ speed ÷ 24
FO used = FO rate × days outside ECA
DO used = DO(ECA) × days in ECA + DO(always) × all days
FW used = (FW use − FW production) × days
Port: rates × port stay (ECA port: diesel instead of fuel oil), then bunkers are added
Corrected displacement = load line displacement, or table Δ at the draft limit × (water density ÷ table S.G.) – the smaller one
Fresh water: produced from day 2 to the last full day of each leg, never above the tank capacity
Non-cargo weight = FO + DO + FW + Ballast + Constant + Others
Cargo allowed at a port = Corrected displacement − Lightship − Non-cargo weight. Maximum cargo = the smallest of all ports.

Deflection (hog / sag)

–
–
–
Deflection = Mid draft − (Fwd + Aft) ÷ 2. A negative value is hogging (midship up), a positive value is sagging. A hogged ship has too much weight at the ends: move cargo towards midship. A sagged ship has too much weight amidships: move cargo towards the ends. This is an indication from the drafts only. Use the loading computer for the bending moment.

Stowage factor

–
–
1 m³/t = 35.881 ft³/long ton. 1 ft³/lt = 0.02787 m³/t.

Stowage factor of a partly filled hold

–
–
Stowage factor = space occupied ÷ cargo weight. The space occupied can come from the ullage or from the cargo height × the hold area. Space left = hold volume − space occupied.

KG of a hold with two stowage factors

–
–
–
Assumes the hold has the same floor area at every level (a box shape). Height of each layer = weight × stowage factor ÷ area. KG of each layer is its half height above the floor. KG = Σ(weight × KG layer) ÷ Σ weight. For a hold with sloping sides use the figures from the capacity plan.

Air draft

–
–
Air draft = height above keel − draft at the mast. Draft at the mast = mean draft + trim × distance ÷ LPP. A rising tide reduces the air draft. Clearance = bridge height − air draft. Allow a safety margin of at least 0.5 to 1 m.

Drafts when listed

–
–
Draft at each side = centreline draft ± (breadth ÷ 2) × tan(list). Under-keel clearance is least on the low side. To find the list from two readings: tan(list) = (stbd draft − port draft) ÷ breadth.

Dew point from the dry bulb with the wet bulb or RH. Ventilate only if the dew point of the outside air is lower than the cargo and hold air temperatures. The cargo's own instruction comes first.
