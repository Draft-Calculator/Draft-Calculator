Privacy Policy

App: Quick Draft Calculator   Developer: Mark Jun   Last updated: 18 October 2026

Summary

Quick Draft Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (vessel particulars, hydrostatic tables, capacity data, voyage plans, notes and calculations) is saved only in the app's local storage on your own device. It never leaves your device and the developer cannot see it.

Backups

The "Backup all data" menu item creates a file on your device. You decide where to keep it or whom to send it to. "Restore backup" reads a file you choose.

Network use

The app works offline. It makes no network requests other than loading its own files from the address it is installed from.

Permissions

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other sensitive permission. Files are read only when you choose them (for example when importing a hydrostatic table).

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. Because no data is held elsewhere, this removes everything.

Children

The app is a professional tool for ship officers and is not directed at children.

Disclaimer

Results are aids to calculation only. The Master and officers remain responsible for checking all figures against the ship's approved stability, loading and hydrostatic documents.
